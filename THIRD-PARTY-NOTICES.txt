dotnet/runtime
--------------
Source: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime (tag v11.0.0, commit deae2e32bf0e018e09d64597b7c59b62b73bb3ce)
Use: Styml is a derivative of System.Text.Json. The library (src/Styml), its source generator
(src/Styml.SourceGeneration) and their shared sources (src/Common) derive from System.Text.Json's src, gen and
Common folders, renamed to System.Text.Yaml and adapted to YAML 1.2. src/Vendored holds files that System.Text.Json
links from dotnet/runtime's shared Common, System.Private.CoreLib and System.Collections sources. The test projects
test/Styml.Tests, test/Styml.SourceGeneration.Tests, test/Styml.SourceGeneration.Unit.Tests,
test/Styml.SourceGeneration.TestLibrary, test/Common and test/Vendored derive from System.Text.Json's tests and
dotnet/runtime's shared test utilities, and docs/upstream holds System.Text.Json's documents. Files that came from
dotnet/runtime keep its file header, modified or not; UPSTREAM.md records the import, the rename and every local
deviation. dotnet/runtime's own third-party notices are kept verbatim in docs/upstream/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.TXT.
